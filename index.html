A few helpful things

Good to know, before you make it yours.

We want the custom process to feel simple and clear. These are the details we will finalize before the store begins accepting real orders.

What kind of pet photo should I upload?

Choose a clear, well-lit photo where your pet's face and distinctive markings are easy to see. Avoid heavy filters, very dark images, or photos where the face is far away.

Can I review the artwork before production?

That is part of the planned PAWMOIRE experience. The current preview is a prototype; final artwork approval and production handoff will be connected before launch.

How long will shipping take?

Shipping times depend on the final product and production partner. We will publish verified processing and delivery estimates for each item before real checkout is enabled.

What if I need to change my personalization?

Personalized items may have different change and return rules. Our final policy will explain the approval window, production cutoff, damaged-item process, and contact route before orders open.

Will I see the design before it is made?

Yes. PAWMOIRE's team will prepare a design proof from your original photo and send it to you for approval. Production starts only after you approve it.

Do I need to remove the photo background?

No. Upload your original photo. Our team will handle the background removal and artwork preparation.

Can I ask for a change?

You can send your notes with the photo. The proof-review and revision policy will be finalized before the store accepts real orders.
